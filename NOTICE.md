# NOTICE

## Word list — Collins Scrabble Words 2021 (CSW21)

Scrabble Babble validates words against **Collins Scrabble Words 2021 (CSW21)**.

- Word list: Collins Scrabble Words 2021 (CSW21).
- Source: https://github.com/scrabblewords/scrabblewords
- CSW21 is © HarperCollins Publishers Limited, published under licence with Collins.

Only the bare words are bundled (`public/csw21.txt.gz`); the source's definitions are
stripped at fetch time by `scripts/fetch-csw21.mjs`. CSW21 is a copyrighted lexicon,
**not** public domain.

This replaced the project's original public-domain ENABLE word list. See
[`docs/IP_DIVERGENCES.md`](docs/IP_DIVERGENCES.md) for the rationale.
